## 2026-09-09: Geometric Reasoning Literature Critique

Reviewed papers across abstract reasoning, contrastive/relational learning,
object-centric visual reasoning, perturbation robustness, geometry question
answering, and neuro-symbolic geometry theorem proving.

Short assessment: the current GRR-001 approach is a good first perturbation
robustness harness, but it is not yet a strong geometry system. Literature
supports pair/contrast-set evaluation and relational inductive bias, but warns
against overclaiming from a single symbolic rail. Stronger geometry systems
use structured representations, symbolic checkers, proof/search components, or
explicit spatial/program geometry benchmarks.
